# Secretary guide

Sign in with the account and private temporary credentials supplied by the Director; local demo accounts were removed. Set a private password when asked. In **Learners**, choose **View profile** to open the permitted learner record; Secretary and Head Teacher can edit learner profiles.

## Admit a learner

1. Open **Admissions** or choose **Full admission** from the learner register.
2. Complete the seven steps: learner details, residence, family contacts, restricted health information, documents, fee hand-off, and consent/review. The draft saves as you go and appears in the Admissions list if you need to resume it.
3. Upload the learner photo and birth certificate when available. Other requested records can be attached from the Documents step. Files are private to authorized school roles.
4. Submit the reviewed record. The school assigns the next `MR-##` number and sends the pending admission to the Head Teacher. Download or print the branded admission form from the confirmation page.
5. If the family needs online access, choose **Create learner login** or select a guardian and choose **Create/link parent login**. A new account has a one-time temporary password; hand it over privately. Existing parent accounts are matched by phone/ID and linked without creating duplicates. Learner and parent access stays suspended until the Head Teacher approves the admission.

The Secretary can edit only their own drafts, view submitted admission details and see the learner's paid/balance summary. The Secretary cannot record, approve or void payments, edit fee structures, review marks, or access discipline records. The family fee note is a hand-off; the Accountant uses the approved fee structure to create the actual invoice.

## What happens after submission

The Head Teacher approves or returns the admission. The Director can review admissions and their audit history. Once approved, any learner or parent logins created for the admission become active; the user changes the temporary password at first sign-in.

Complaints are submitted by parents directly to the Head Teacher and/or Director. The Secretary does not view or manage complaint threads.
