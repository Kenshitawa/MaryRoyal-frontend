# Head Teacher guide

Use the account and private temporary credentials supplied by the Director; local demo accounts were removed. Change the temporary password at first sign-in. In **CBC assessments**, review submitted marks, approve or return them with a reason. Final release is a Director action; this is assessment-entry review, not a complete generated report-card workflow.

In **Learners**, choose **View profile** to open biography, attendance and other permitted sections. Head Teacher and Secretary can edit learner profiles; the Director oversees records without learner-edit permission. The daily attendance register, staff directory and audit log are available for oversight. Learner/report exports remain Director-only. Fee-structure management exists, but current academic, account-provisioning and privacy gaps in `docs/ROADMAP.md` block production release.
